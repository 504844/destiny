How to use

There are two ways to show the cards on stream. Start with the link. Keep the GIF for when the link stutters.

Link Use this

Shows the cards live. After you press Save, they update on stream by themselves within about 10 seconds.

GIF Backup

A moving picture of the cards. Use it only if the link stutters. It doesn't update by itself.

1. Add the overlay to TikTok LIVE Studio

Only once
  1. Under Links for TikTok LIVE Studio above, click Copy next to All in one.
  2. In Studio, click Add source on the left and choose Link. Click into the Link box and paste with Ctrl+V.
  3. Turn on Custom resolution and type 1080 in W and 1000 in H. Leave Always keep active on, then click Add source.
  4. Click the overlay on the phone screen so a red box appears. In the bar above the phone, click the small arrow next to the four-arrows button, then Fit.
  5. Drag the overlay up until the number above the red box shows 230 px. That leaves the gap for the song player.
Good to know
  • Only move the overlay. Pulling the corners makes it blurry.
  • The space between the cards is see-through, so your camera shows through.

2. Change prices, commands or gigs

Any time, even live
  1. Type your changes in the boxes above. The preview next to each card shows how it will look.
  2. Add rows with + Add price, + Add command or + Add gig. Remove a row with ×, move it with the arrows.
  3. Press Save at the top, or Ctrl+S. The stream shows the change within about 10 seconds. You don't need to touch Studio.
Good to know
  • Up to 3 gigs. They sort by date, and each one hides itself the day after it happens.
  • To hide a card, turn off Show on stream at its top right.
  • Changed your mind before saving? Undo changes brings back the last saved version.

3. Use a GIF if the link stutters

Backup
  1. Get everything right here first. The GIF shows what's on screen right now, saved or not.
  2. Under Links for TikTok LIVE Studio, click Download GIF next to All in one. After a few seconds it says Downloaded, and dj-destiny-all.gif is in your Downloads.
  3. In Studio, turn off or remove the Link source with the overlay.
  4. Click Add source, choose Image, and pick dj-destiny-all.gif.
  5. Place it like the link: click it, choose Fit, and drag it up to 230 px.
Keep in mind
  • A GIF doesn't update by itself. After you change something, or after a gig date has passed, download a new one and replace the old one in Studio.
  • In the GIF the cards are solid dark instead of slightly see-through.
  • To go back, turn the Link source on again and remove the GIF.

Something looks wrong

Didn't change after Save
Wait 10 seconds. Still old? In Studio, click the overlay, then the round arrow (refresh) above the phone.
The overlay is blurry
It was resized. Do steps 4 and 5 of part 1 again.
A card is missing
Check that Show on stream is on for that card, then press Save.
The overlay stutters
Switch to a GIF, see part 3.
GIF button says "Nothing to show"
That card is hidden, or all its gigs have passed.
It says the PIN is wrong
Ask the person who set this up for the new PIN. Your typed changes stay on screen.
Red "Could not save" at the top
Check your internet and press Save again.
