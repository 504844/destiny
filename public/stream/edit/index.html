How to use

A. Put the overlay into TikTok LIVE Studio (only once)

  1. Under Links for TikTok LIVE Studio above, click Copy next to All in one.
  2. Open TikTok LIVE Studio. On the left, click Add source, then choose Link.
  3. Click into the Link box and paste (Ctrl+V).
  4. Turn on Custom resolution. Type 1080 in W and 1000 in H.
  5. Leave Always keep active on. Click Add source.
  6. Click the overlay on the phone screen so a red box appears around it.
  7. In the bar above the phone, click the small arrow next to the button with four corner arrows, then click Fit.
  8. Drag the overlay up until the number above the red box shows 230 px. Now the cards are in the right place, with the gap for the song player.
  9. Don't pull the corners to make it bigger or smaller, or it gets blurry. Only move it.

The space between the cards is see-through, so your camera shows through it.

B. Change prices, commands or gigs (any time, even during a live)

  1. Open this page and enter the PIN if it asks. It remembers you on this device.
  2. Type your changes into the boxes. The preview next to each card shows how it will look.
  3. To add or remove a price, command or gig, use + Add price, + Add command, + Add gig, or the × on a row. The arrows move a row up or down.
  4. Gigs: you can list up to 3. They sort by date, and a gig disappears from the stream by itself the day after it happens, so you never have to delete old ones before going live.
  5. With 2 or 3 gigs, the card shows one gig at a time and rolls to the next. Under Gigs, choose how long each one shows: 7, 14 or 28 seconds.
  6. To hide a card from the stream, turn off Show on stream at the top right of that card.
  7. Press Save at the top. After about 10 seconds the overlay in TikTok LIVE Studio changes by itself. You don't need to touch Studio.
  8. Changed your mind before saving? Press Undo changes.

C. If something looks wrong

  • Overlay didn't change after Save: wait 10 seconds. Still old? In Studio, click the overlay, then click the round arrow (refresh) in the bar above the phone.
  • Overlay is blurry: it was resized. Do steps 7 and 8 from part A again.
  • A card is missing: check that Show on stream is on for that card here, then press Save.
  • Overlay stutters on stream: use a GIF instead, see part D.
  • It says the PIN is wrong: ask the person who set this up for the new PIN. Your typed changes stay on the screen while you enter it.
  • Red "Could not save" at the top: check your internet and press Save again.

D. Use a GIF instead of the link (only if the overlay stutters)

  1. Set everything up here first. The GIF shows what's on this page right now, saved or not.
  2. Under Links for TikTok LIVE Studio, click Download GIF next to All in one. Wait until it says Downloaded. The file dj-destiny-all.gif is now in your Downloads.
  3. In TikTok LIVE Studio, turn off or remove the Link source with the overlay.
  4. On the left, click Add source, choose Image, and pick dj-destiny-all.gif.
  5. Place it like in part A: click it, click Fit (step 7), and drag it up to 230 px (step 8).

A GIF doesn't update by itself. After you change something here, or after a gig date has passed, download a new GIF and put it in Studio instead of the old one. In a GIF the cards are solid dark instead of slightly see-through. If the button says "Nothing to show", that card is hidden or all its gigs have passed.
